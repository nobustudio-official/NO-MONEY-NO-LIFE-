## 生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one production-ready transparent PNG map object for the Japanese fantasy board game NO MONEY, NO LIFE.
Primary request: a charming royal castle with a small central forecourt, designed to sit directly on the game's existing pale blue-gray paved capital terrain.
Style: polished hand-painted Japanese chibi RPG / party-board-game art, 2D illustration with convincing toy-like 3D volume. Rounded substantial architecture, crisp warm dark contours, clean cel-shaded planes with gentle painterly highlights, restrained large readable details. Match a cute anime chibi cleric with white-and-royal-blue costume, gold trim, vivid clean colors. Do not depict the cleric or any characters.
Camera is essential: fixed orthographic view looking down from approximately 35–40 degrees above the ground, front-facing castle entrance pointing toward the bottom of the image. Roof tops and the top of the courtyard must be clearly visible, while the front walls remain tall and visible. Near-symmetric front view, no dramatic perspective convergence, no horizon, not top-down, not a low eye-level architectural painting, and NOT a 45-degree diamond-isometric camera. Width axis mostly horizontal and depth recedes toward the top of the image.
Subject: one cohesive compact castle complex, a central keep and a few stout rounded turret towers with royal blue roofs, cream-white masonry, tasteful gold finials and a simple royal crest. A generous welcoming central arched gateway, short side wings and enclosing walls. A modest open forecourt immediately in front, pale cool gray paving matching the capital, leaving the approach to the entrance visually open for routes to meet it later. Castle is the focus, distinct bold silhouette readable at about 300–500 pixels wide. Avoid hundreds of tiny spires, excessive windows or fine engraving.
Lighting: consistent daylight from upper left; softly shaded right-facing walls and a subtle short contact shadow cast toward lower right, emphasizing contact with flat map ground.
Composition: a single complete standalone object centered on canvas with comfortable transparent margins, no cropping of towers or footprint. Castle and its modest forecourt only. Ground paving ends naturally around the compact footprint; no raised diorama pedestal, no floating island, no surrounding landscape, no ocean, no sky, no backdrop, no display base. Real alpha transparency outside the object, no checkerboard painted into the image. No text, labels, UI, border, watermark, people or characters.
